          Before you start

          This is a sketching tool. Spine Builder is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. What you build here is a picture to think and talk with. It is not a measurement and not a diagnosis. Take questions about your own spine to the people who treat you.

          Your sketch stays with you. There are no accounts and no tracking. This page does not send what you build anywhere, and it carries a rule that stops it from contacting any server once it is open. Files you open are read by your own browser. A share link carries the sketch inside the link itself, so it goes only where you send it.

          What is kept on this device. Two small settings: how you set the look, and whether you ticked the box below. Nothing about your spine is kept. Close the page without saving and the sketch is gone.

          Like any website, the service that hosts this page can see that a device asked for the page. It does not see what you do here.

          Presets

          Pick the one nearest to your own spine and shape it from there. It replaces what is on screen, so save a copy first if you want to keep that. These are made-up shapes of common patterns. They are not a diagnosis.
