# Privacy

Spine Builder is a set of plain files that run in your browser. This is everything there is
to say about what happens to your information.

## What leaves your device

Nothing you build, open or type. The app has no accounts, no tracking, no advertising and no
server of its own. It makes no requests after the page has loaded.

The page also carries a rule (a content security policy) that forbids it to contact any
server, to post a form or to load anything from another site. Your browser enforces that
rule. The rule names the app's own script by its fingerprint, so a copy whose script was
changed on the way does not run.

## What is kept on your device

Two small settings, in the browser's storage for this site:

- how you set the look (shading, background and colours),
- whether you ticked "Do not show this again" on the opening note.

Nothing about your spine is kept. A sketch exists only in the open page until you save it as
a file, a link, a picture or an STL file, and those go where you put them. Clearing the
site's data in your browser removes the two settings.

There are no cookies.

## Share links

A share link holds the whole sketch inside the address, in the part after the # sign.
Browsers do not send that part to a web server. Whoever has the link can open the sketch,
with its name, its pain points and its notes. Send it only to people who should see those.

## The hosting service

A hosted copy sits on a web host. Like any website, that host can see that a device asked
for the page: the address of the device, the time and the kind of browser. It does not see
what you do on the page. If you would rather not have even that, download the app and open
it from a folder. It works without an internet connection.

## Pictures and X-rays

A picture of an X-ray that you open to trace over is read by your own browser and held in
the memory of the page. It is not sent anywhere, it is not kept on the device, and it is not
part of the sketch: a saved file, a share link and a saved picture hold nothing of it. Close
the page and it is gone.

Pictures saved from the app hold only the model and its labels. If you later share an X-ray, check
its edges first. X-rays often carry a name and a date of birth in the margins.

## Not medical advice

Spine Builder is a sketching tool. It is not a medical device, it does not measure and it
does not diagnose.
