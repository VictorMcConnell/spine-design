The short version. Spine Builder is a free tool for sketching a spine. It is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Your sketches are yours and stay on your device. The tool is offered as it is, with no promise that it is right for any purpose.

1. What Spine Builder is

Spine Builder is a free, open source tool that runs in your browser. With it you shape a 3D sketch of a spine: its curves, a fused part, rods, screws, hooks, pain points and notes. It is run by the Spine Builder project. By using the site you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use it.

2. Not a medical device, not medical advice

  • Spine Builder is a sketching tool. It does not measure, diagnose, treat or plan treatment.
  • A sketch is a picture to think and talk with. It is not a medical image and not a record of anyone's body, even when it was shaped to look like a real spine.
  • The angles and other numbers the app shows describe the sketch, not a person.
  • Do not base a decision about your health on a sketch. Take questions about your own spine to the people who treat you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

3. Your sketches

What you make is yours. It stays on your device unless you save it or send it. A link, a picture or a file that you pass on carries what is in the sketch, including its notes and pain points, so you decide who gets to see those. The privacy page says what the site does and does not keep.

4. Using the site fairly

  • Do not present a sketch as a medical image, a measurement or a scan of a real person.
  • Do not try to break the site, overload it or get around its safety rules.
  • Do not use the name of the project to suggest that it supports a product, a clinic or a treatment.
