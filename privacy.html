Privacy

Last changed on 16 November 2026

The short version. Spine-Design runs in your browser. Nothing you build, open or type is sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no advertising.

Who runs this site

This site is run by the Spine-Design project, an open source project. Its code is public at github.com/VictorMcConnell/spine-design, so everything on this page can be checked there.

What leaves your device

Nothing you build, open or type. The app has no accounts and no server of its own. It makes no requests after the page has loaded.

The page also carries a rule (a content security policy) that forbids it to contact any server, to post a form or to load anything from another site. Your browser enforces that rule. The rule names the app's own script by its fingerprint, so a copy whose script was changed on the way does not run.

What is kept on your device

Two small settings, in the browser's storage for this site:

  • how you set the look (shading, background and colours),
  • whether you ticked "Do not show this again" on the opening note.

Nothing about your spine is kept. A sketch exists only in the open page until you save it as a file, a link, a picture or a file for a 3D printer, and those go where you put them. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes the two settings.

There are no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

Share links

A share link holds the whole sketch inside the address, in the part after the # sign. Browsers do not send that part to a web server. Whoever has the link can open the sketch, with its name, its pain points and its notes. Send it only to people who should see those.

A sketch on someone else's page

Share can make a few lines of code that show a sketch on another website, in a small viewer. The sketch is inside that code, so anyone who can see the page can see the sketch. The viewer is loaded from this site and works the same way as the app: it sends nothing anywhere and keeps nothing on the device. The page around it cannot reach into the viewer, and the viewer cannot reach into the page. The host of this site can see that a device asked for the viewer, as with any page, and may be told by the browser which page it was shown on.

The hosting service

This site sits on a web host. Like any website, that host can see that a device asked for the page: the address of the device, the time and the kind of browser. It keeps that in its own logs under its own privacy terms. It does not see what you do on the page. If you would rather not have even that, download the app and open it from a folder. It works without an internet connection.

Pictures and X-rays

A picture of an X-ray that you open to trace over is read by your own browser and held in the memory of the page. It is not sent anywhere, it is not kept on the device, and it is not part of the sketch: a saved file, a share link and a saved picture hold nothing of it. Close the page and it is gone.

Pictures saved from the app hold only the model and its labels. If you share an X-ray somewhere else, check its edges first. X-rays often carry a name and a date of birth in the margins.

Children

There is nothing to sign up for and nothing is collected, from anyone of any age.

Your rights

Privacy laws give people the right to see, correct and delete what is held about them. The project holds nothing about you, so there is nothing to look up or delete. The two settings on your own device are yours to clear at any time.

Changes to this page

If the app starts to work differently, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.

Questions

Ask on the project's page for questions and problems: github.com/VictorMcConnell/spine-design/issues. That page is public, so leave out personal and medical details.
